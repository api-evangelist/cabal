# Cabal

> AI-powered relationship intelligence. Ask who's connected to anyone. Cabal turns an organization's professional relationships into warm introductions, inferring connections across millions of professional records without CSV uploads or calendar syncs. Query it through in-app chat, Claude and ChatGPT via MCP, a Slack bot, or an API.

Cabal is a relationship-intelligence and warm-introduction platform for VC platform / deal-sourcing teams, sales organizations, and investor-relations / partnerships teams. It also ships "Cabal Mail" for bulk personalized outreach (company updates, LP communications, strategic asks). Backed by Craft Ventures.

## Products
- [Cabal for Sales](https://getcabal.com/companies): Generate pipeline and accelerate deals through executive, investor, and advisor networks.
- [Cabal for VCs](https://getcabal.com/investors): Support portfolio companies with introductions and track assistance impact.
- [Cabal Mail](https://getcabal.com/mail): Bulk personalized outreach — updates, LP comms, strategic asks.

## Access channels
- API: programmatic access to the relationship-intelligence graph.
- MCP: query Cabal from Claude and ChatGPT via the Model Context Protocol.
- Slack bot: ask relationship questions in Slack.
- In-app chat.
